Does sustainability leadership pay on the Nigerian Exchange?

We build evidence-based benchmarks that turn ESG performance data into something investors can actually allocate against.

Download the white paper See the ESG 20 Index

Working Paper 2026-01. Backtested 4 January 2022 to 24 July 2026 using publicly disclosed ESG ratings and NGX price and dividend data.

What the first study found

A portfolio of the twenty highest publicly rated ESG performers on the Nigerian Exchange, held through the period and rebalanced annually, outpaced every major domestic benchmark while drawing down less in the worst stretch.

Featured publication

Working Paper 2026-01  ·  August 2026

Can sustainable leaders on the Nigerian Exchange outperform the market?

The paper sets out an investable ESG benchmark for Nigeria: how constituents are selected from publicly disclosed ratings, how the portfolio is weighted and rebalanced, what the 2022–2026 backtest shows, and where the result survives or breaks under eight robustness tests.

Research themes

Who this is for

Pension fund administrators and asset managers looking for a domestic sustainability benchmark. Regulators and exchanges weighing disclosure reform. Researchers who want the method open enough to test.
