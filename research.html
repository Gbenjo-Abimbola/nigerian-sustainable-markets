Working Paper 2026-01August 2026
Capital markets

Can sustainable leaders on the Nigerian Exchange outperform the market?

Constructs an investable ESG benchmark from publicly disclosed ratings of NGX-listed issuers and tests it against the All Share Index, NGX 30 and NGX Banking Index over four and a half years. Reports return, risk, drawdown and attribution, and re-runs the result under eight robustness variants covering rebalancing frequency, constituent count, removal of the largest individual holdings and removal of the banking sector.
