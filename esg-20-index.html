The ESG 20 Index

A rules-based portfolio of twenty NGX-listed companies with the strongest publicly disclosed ESG ratings, built to test whether sustainability leadership shows up in shareholder returns.

What drove it

Banks occupy nine of the twenty seats, yet the excess return is concentrated in non-bank names — Seplat Energy, BUA Foods and Lafarge Africa in particular. Strip the banking sector out entirely and the portfolio still beats all three benchmarks, which matters because it means the result is not simply a repackaged bet on Nigerian banks.

Where it is fragile

The window covers an exceptional period for Nigerian equities, including the naira devaluation and the repricing that followed. The sample is one market over four and a half years, the ratings universe is limited to issuers that have been rated, and liquidity on some constituents would constrain how much capital could track the portfolio in practice.

From research to a live benchmark

The index runs as a published paper portfolio first. Each stage has to hold before the next one starts.

  1. Publish the method and the evidence

    The working paper sets out selection, weighting, rebalancing and the full backtest, so the result can be reproduced and argued with.

  2. Track it quarterly in public

    A live paper record builds the out-of-sample history that any institutional allocator will ask for before taking a new benchmark seriously.

  3. Independent calculation and governance

    An index committee, a written rulebook and third-party calculation, with treatment for corporate actions, suspensions and rating changes between rebalances.

  4. Licensing to managers and exchanges

    Licensed to asset managers and pension fund administrators for benchmarking and product construction, with the index data and constituent analytics available by subscription.

Constituent universe

Selection draws on ESG ratings already published for NGX-listed issuers. Sector representation in the current list looks like this:

  • Banking — 9
  • Industrial goods — 3
  • Consumer goods — 3
  • Energy — 2
  • Telecoms — 1
  • Agriculture — 1
  • Insurance — 1

The full constituent list, weights and return series are in the working paper and available on request.
